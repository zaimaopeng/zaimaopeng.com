## Overview

CranioSwift is an AI-powered system designed to revolutionize the way cranial implants are designed and manufactured. This project received an **AEA Ignite Grant of $485,000** to bring it from concept to reality.

## The Problem

Traditional cranial implant design is:
- **Time-consuming** — Takes weeks to design and iterate
- **Expensive** — Requires specialized expertise
- **Limited** — Doesn't leverage modern AI capabilities

## Our Solution

CranioSwift uses machine learning to:
1. Analyze CT scan data
2. Generate optimized implant designs
3. Prepare files for 3D printing
4. Dramatically reduce design-to-production time
